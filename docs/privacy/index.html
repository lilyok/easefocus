Privacy Policy

Pomodoro Planner keeps goals, plans, survey answers, and focus history on your device. The app does not track you and does not use analytics.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Data collection

Pomodoro Planner does not collect personal data for analytics or advertising, does not require an account, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

On-device data

Tasks, plans, timer settings, and session history are stored locally (including in easefocus.store). They are not uploaded to a developer backend.

Apple Intelligence

When Apple Intelligence is available, plan generation and quote selection can run on device. Apple Intelligence is optional; manual tasks and the timer work without it.

Optional quote list fetch

The app may fetch a public JSON quote list over HTTPS and merge new attributed quotes into the local library. That request does not send your account, tasks, or plans.

Optional Google search

If you confirm Search Google, only the query you reviewed opens in your system browser. Nothing is sent to Google during plan generation or when you save a plan.
